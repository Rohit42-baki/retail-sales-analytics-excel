I analysed 4,200 retail orders (2020–2024) in Excel to answer one question: what really drives profit?

 

🔎 What I found

 • Electronics = 60% of sales but only 39% of profit

 • 12 of 42 products make 76% of profit

 • Since 2022, orders fell while order value rose: growth now rests on fewer, larger orders

 • 98% of customers bought only once

 • ~20% of reported sales sits on returned or cancelled orders

 

🛠 What I did: cleaned messy data (duplicates, impossible values, mixed dates), flagged invalid rows instead of deleting them, built KPIs, 25+ PivotTables and slicers, and a dashboard. I also reconciled every headline number against an independent check.

 

💡 Biggest lesson: sales rank can mislead. Chennai ranks #1 by sales but 18th of 20 cities by margin.
