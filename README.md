I analyzed 4,200 retail orders from 2020-2024 to understand what drives profitability.

• Gross Sales reached ₹232.5M, generating ₹43M profit at an 18.5% margin.
 • Order volume peaked in 2022 and has declined since, while Average Order Value continued to rise, indicating growth is increasingly dependent on fewer but larger orders.
 • Electronics generated the largest share of sales but delivered one of the lowest profit margins, suggesting revenue does not always translate into profitability.
 • Regional performance varies significantly: South leads in sales, while Central achieves the highest profit margin.
 • Sales are highly seasonal, with summer months (May-August) contributing the strongest revenue and November-January being the weakest period.
 • Order fulfillment remains a challenge, with approximately 23% of orders ending in returns or cancellations, reducing realized revenue.
